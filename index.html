        Still wondering?

        Are you an e-sport or gaming agency?

        No. Gaming, streaming and e-sport are a special strength, but we work across the entire creator market — lifestyle, fashion, beauty, sport, tech, entertainment, food and travel.

        What size of creators do you work with?

        All sizes. We select by fit, not by follower count. Depending on the goal, a group of focused niche creators can deliver more than one large account — and vice versa.

        Is there a minimum budget for brands?

        We look at every request individually. Tell us your goals and range — we’ll be honest about what is realistic and suggest the most effective set-up.

        I’m a creator — do I have to sign exclusively?

        Not necessarily. We offer full representation as well as project-based collaborations. Which model fits is something we decide together.

        Do you work with teams, events and rights holders?

        Yes. We help teams, athletes, events, publishers and organisations package their assets, find suitable sponsors and activate partnerships — often together with creators.
