    ( 10 ) FAQ

    Good
    questions.

    Are you an e-sport or gaming agency?

    No. Gaming, streaming and e-sport are a special strength of ours, but we work across the entire creator market — from lifestyle, fashion and beauty to sport, tech, entertainment, food and travel.

    What size of creators do you work with?

    All sizes. We select by fit, not by follower count. Depending on the objective, a group of focused niche creators can deliver more than a single large account — and vice versa.

    Is there a minimum budget for brands?

    We look at every request individually. Tell us about your goals and range in the contact form — we will be honest about what is realistic and suggest the most effective set-up.

    I’m a creator — do I have to sign exclusively?

    Not necessarily. We offer both full representation and project-based collaborations. Which model fits is something we decide together in a first conversation.

    Do you work with teams, events and rights holders?

    Yes. We help teams, athletes, events, publishers and organisations package their assets, find suitable sponsors and activate partnerships — often in combination with creators.

    Do you work internationally?

    Yes. We are set up for campaigns across markets and languages, and we coordinate creators and partners wherever your audience is.
